# Ada Bridge Institute — Nairobi site visit

Two days on site with the Ada Bridge team.

- **Completion is strong.** The one-year program holds most of its cohort to graduation.
- **The leak is employment linkage.** The gap between graduating and landing a first
  paid role is where alumni fall out of the earnings trajectory we modeled.
- **What's working:** a stipend bridge covering the search period, plus an
  employer-matching pilot with a handful of Nairobi tech firms. First-role placement
  rate is up 14 points since these launched.
- Alumni who do land roles are earning up to roughly 6x the regional average within
  two years, consistent with the projection.

**Main risk.** The primary risk on this grant is the graduation-to-employment gap: strong
completion does not convert to paid roles fast enough, which puts the projected earnings
effect at risk. The stipend bridge and employer-matching pilot are the mitigations.

Recommend we fund the employer-matching component more deliberately in any renewal.
