# Riverbend Care Collective — Year 1 Progress Report

**Reporting period:** Jan–Dec Year 1
**Grant:** GD-1188 · Powering Economic Opportunity Fund

## Placements

We placed **2,610 caregivers** with families in Year 1, approximately 67% of our
two-year target of 3,900. Placement volume was lower than planned in Q2 because of
Medicaid reimbursement processing delays in two states, which slowed our ability to
onboard new families. We expect to recover this in Year 2.

## Wages

Median wage at placement was **$19.10/hour**, compared with an estimated regional
baseline of **$16.80/hour** for comparable roles.

## Benefits navigation

1,940 placed workers completed at least one benefits-navigation session.

## Year 2 plan

Resume the planned onboarding ramp as state reimbursement issues resolve; deepen the
benefits-navigation offering.
