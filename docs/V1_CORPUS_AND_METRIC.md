# Compass — V1 corpus definition & success metric

Roadmap 1.8. Agreed with the COO and the Chief Programs & Partnerships Officer **with the
full source map in front of them** (Round 0 of discovery), not as a default.

---

## The V1 corpus

**Three systems.** Zoom is deferred; email, Notion, and the impact model are Phase-3+ candidates.

| System | Scope | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **GivingData** | All grants and requests from the corpus start date (proposed: the later of 2022-01-01 or the GivingData migration date). Grants, organizations, contacts, requirements, review notes, declined applicants. | The structured spine everything else reconciles against |
| **Google Drive** | 2–3 named Shared Drives holding grant proposals, reports, diligence memos, site-visit notes, and thesis documents. Named in discovery step D4 from a folder-tree export. **Personal `My Drive` is excluded.** | Where the reasoning lives |
| **Airtable** | The one canonical "Relationships" base — Organizations, Contacts, Interactions — filtered to grantee/co-funder/partner records. | Relationship + sourcing context GivingData doesn't model |

**Corpus start date** matters: if "five years" predates the GivingData migration, part of
the history is in an export from the prior system, and the coverage statement names the cutoff.

**Explicitly not in V1** (and Compass says so on every answer): grants before the start
date, personal drives, Zoom Chat, email, board/HR/compensation/legal material, individual
participant data, un-tagged historical grants.

---

## The 6-month success metric

Agreed with the COO / CPPO. Compass is worth continuing past the pilot if, at 6 months:

1. **Adoption:** ≥ 60% of the Programs + Impact team have used it in the last month, and
   ≥ 3 people use it weekly.
2. **Trust:** on the gold set, citation accuracy ≥ 90% and **zero permission-leak findings**
   in the standing red-team. Users report they verify Compass answers against the cited
   source and find them correct.
3. **Time:** a measurable reduction in the time to prepare for a renewal decision or pull a
   cross-portfolio outcome — baselined against the "before" timing sheet from discovery
   step 1.9 (target: a renewal-prep task that took ~half a day takes ~1 hour).
4. **Honesty:** the team can point to cases where Compass correctly said "I don't have
   that" — and trusts it *more* for doing so, not less.

If (2) is not met, Compass does not go past the pilot regardless of (1) and (3). The
security exit criteria are never traded away.

---

## Phase-end decision points

Each phase ends **continue / change / stop** (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). The COO's office
holds the decision. "Change" usually means: narrow the corpus, narrow the user group, or
extend a phase — never skip a security exit criterion.
