## 1 · The strategic context (from your notes) — this reframes everything

**The Foundation is restructuring.** It's shifting **from causal-based to fund-based
grantmaking**, and scaling from **~$20M/year to $60M+** by **aggregating capital from
multiple funders into pooled distributions**. It's diversifying off GitLab stock (its
primary funder) via this new fund model.

What that means for your pitch:
- The portfolio is about to get **bigger, more complex, and multi-funder** — more grants,
  more co-funders with their own reporting needs and data-use terms, more cross-fund
  questions. "Re-deriving the same portfolio answer every week" gets worse at 3x the scale.
  Compass is **infrastructure for the version of the Foundation they're becoming**, not the
  one they are.
- **Multi-funder pooled capital** = contractual data-use constraints everywhere (Ballmer,
  Annie E. Casey, and now more). Your permission model + `never-ingest` list + co-funder
  handling isn't a nice-to-have — it's a precondition for operating the new model.
- The **grantmaking-as-a-service line of business** (they're already doing this with the
  **OpenAI Foundation — helping distribute $300M in grants** — and want to "turn it into a
  line of business"): internal tools that work become **products they sell to peer
  foundations**. Compass's "swap the connectors, same architecture" design is exactly that
  shape. Name it.

**They are AI-native by identity.** The Foundation *"launched the same month as ChatGPT."*
AI adoption is *"a core priority"* — but the problem is **fragmentation, not resistance**:
> - *"No consolidation across AI tools — team members building independently, no visibility
>   across the org."*
> - *"Wide skill gap — some staff are power users, others treat ChatGPT like Google."*
> - *"Tools in place (Notion, Lattice, Claude, GPT) all have AI features, but nothing is
>   streamlined."*
> - An **internal proposal evaluator a team member built** is *"functional for its creator
>   but not designed for broader use — flagged as a vibe-code problem."*
> - An **Impact Insights Generator** queries *"aggregated data across 200 grantees; useful
>   but needs refinement."*

**This is your opening.** The job is not "add AI." It's **consolidate, make it legible,
bring the whole team along, and make it last.** Every one of those is something you can
speak to concretely.
